Messages the floor manager sends into the room.

Copied verbatim from the world's Config. <PLACEHOLDERS> are filled in before
sending, and the [TAG] in square brackets is stripped before the message is
shown to you.

Most of these never reach your processor. Your guest code prints them to your
console and drops them, so they are not in the transcript your processor reads.
The exceptions are:

  Config.survey_message / survey_message_nobody
      the vote request, which does enter the transcript as a MANAGER line

  "Dear <YOUR_NAME>, open the conversation naturally."
      the opening nudge, which is not in this file because your guest code
      generates it locally when the room starts

Those two are the only MANAGER lines you will ever see inside a prompt.
